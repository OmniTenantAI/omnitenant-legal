OmniTenant · property-management software

OmniTenant is used by landlord and property-management companies to manage properties, leases, invoices, payments and maintenance requests. If you are a tenant of one of those companies, or a member of its staff, you can choose to receive text message notifications about your own account.

This is optional. Your account works normally without it, and you will still receive email notifications.

Step 1 — Your mobile number
What you will receive. Only messages about activity on your own account:
  • Maintenance request status changes
  • Notice that an invoice has been issued, and its due date
  • Confirmation that a payment was received
  • Reminders that a lease is approaching its end date
  • For staff: an alert that a tenant support conversation needs a person
No marketing or promotional messages are ever sent. Most recipients receive fewer than 10 messages per month.

Reply STOP to any message from OmniTenant and all texts stop immediately. Reply HELP for assistance, or email andrew.ahr51@gmail.com. Opting out of text messages does not close your account and does not stop email notifications.
