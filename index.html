OmniTenant

Property-management software for landlords and property managers.

OmniTenant is a web application used by landlord and property-management companies to run their rental business, and by the tenants of those companies to manage their own tenancy. Each company's records are held in a separate database from every other company's.

What the software does

For the landlord or property-management company:

  • Property and unit records, including photographs
  • Owner records and property assignment
  • Tenant records and lease lifecycle — draft, active, renewal, termination
  • Rent invoicing, including automatic monthly generation and prorating
  • Rent collection by card and bank transfer, with accounting synchronisation
  • Maintenance requests, vendor assignment and job costing

For the tenant:

  • View their lease, invoices and payment history
  • Pay rent online
  • Raise and follow maintenance requests
  • Message their landlord or property manager for support

Text message notifications

OmniTenant sends transactional text messages only, and only to people who have entered their own mobile number, ticked a consent box, and confirmed the number with a one-time code. No marketing or promotional messages are sent, and mobile information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Recipients are the tenants and staff of a landlord organization that uses OmniTenant. Messages relate only to activity on the recipient's own account: maintenance request status changes, notice that an invoice has been issued and its due date, confirmation that a payment was received, reminders that a lease is nearing its end date, and — for staff — an alert that a tenant support conversation needs a person.

Message frequency varies with activity on the account; most recipients receive fewer than 10 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts, or HELP for assistance. Opting out of text messages does not close the account and does not stop email notifications.
